HealthPro Widgets

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Privacy at a glance: HealthPro Widgets is designed to read and display the Apple Health data you choose to authorize. Your health measurements are processed on your device and are not uploaded to a HealthPro Widgets server. No HealthPro Widgets account is required.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how HealthPro Widgets ("HealthPro", "we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use the app, its Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and its Apple Watch widgets.

2. Apple Health and HealthKit data

HealthPro Widgets can access health and fitness information stored in Apple Health through Apple's HealthKit framework. The app only requests access to health data types that you choose to enable, and Apple asks you for permission before HealthPro can access them.

Depending on the features and health values you enable, this may include information such as:

  • activity data, including step count, walking and running distance, flights climbed, exercise time, stand time, and energy burned;
  • walking, mobility, running, wheelchair, and gait measurements;
  • workout and sports information, such as workout duration, cycling, swimming, running, rowing, skiing, paddling, and related measurements;
  • heart information, such as heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, recovery measurements, atrial fibrillation burden, and ECG-related values when supported by your device;
  • sleep information, including sleep analysis and supported sleep-related measurements;
  • vital signs such as oxygen saturation and respiratory rate; and
  • supported environment and exposure measurements such as audio exposure, time in daylight, and water temperature.

The exact health data types requested are shown in Apple's Health permission interface. You can choose which permissions to grant, and you can change those permissions later.

3. How health data is used

HealthPro uses the health data you authorize only to provide app functionality, including displaying health and activity information in the app and in widgets, preparing widget values, and refreshing those displays.

HealthPro does not use HealthKit data for advertising, marketing, user profiling, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell HealthKit data, and we do not share HealthKit data with data brokers, advertising networks, or other third parties.

4. On-device storage and widget data

HealthPro may store app settings, widget selections, entitlement state, and temporary or cached values locally on your device so that the app and its widgets can function correctly. Some health values may therefore be copied into local app storage or a local App Group container used by the app's widget extensions.

These locally stored values remain on your device. HealthPro does not upload your health measurements to a developer-controlled HealthPro server.

5. Subscriptions and purchases

If you purchase or restore HealthPro Pro, the purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. HealthPro may receive limited information from Apple that is necessary to determine your subscription or entitlement status, such as whether Pro access is active or whether a purchase has been restored.

We do not receive or store your full payment card information. Apple's handling of App Store purchase and account information is governed by Apple's own privacy policies and terms.

6. Accounts, advertising, and analytics

  • No HealthPro account is required.
  • HealthPro does not sell your personal information or health information.
  • HealthPro does not use your HealthKit data for advertising or marketing.
  • HealthPro does not use your HealthKit data to track you across apps or websites.
  • HealthPro does not share your HealthKit data with third-party analytics services.

7. Data sharing

We do not share your HealthKit health measurements with third parties. Apple provides the operating-system services used for HealthKit permissions, widgets, Apple Watch integration, and App Store purchases.

8. Data retention and deletion

Because HealthPro does not upload your health measurements to a HealthPro server, we do not maintain a server-side copy of your HealthKit data.

Local app settings and cached widget information may remain on your device while the app is installed. You can remove this local app data by deleting HealthPro Widgets from your device. Your original health information remains in Apple Health and is controlled through Apple Health and your device settings.

App Store transaction records may continue to be retained by Apple according to Apple's own policies, even after you delete the app.

9. Your choices and permissions

You control HealthPro's access to Apple Health data. You can review or revoke Health access at any time using the Health app or the applicable Privacy & Security settings on your Apple device.

If you revoke access to a health data type, HealthPro will no longer be able to read that data type. Previously cached local widget values may remain temporarily until they are refreshed, replaced, or the app is removed.

10. Security

HealthPro is designed to minimize data collection by keeping health processing and widget data on your device. Your HealthKit data is also protected by Apple's platform security and HealthKit permission controls.

11. Children

HealthPro Widgets is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through a HealthPro-operated account or server. Health data access remains subject to Apple's device, account, and HealthKit controls.

12. Medical disclaimer

HealthPro Widgets is intended to provide a convenient display of health and activity information. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition, and it should not be used for emergency or medical decision-making.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when HealthPro's features or data practices change. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date shown at the top of this page. Material changes to how health or personal data is handled will be reflected in an updated policy.

14. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or HealthPro Widgets' privacy practices, please contact us using the developer support contact provided on the HealthPro Widgets App Store listing.

This policy applies to HealthPro Widgets and its associated widgets on supported Apple devices.
