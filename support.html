Widget Help

If a widget is not showing the expected health information:

  • Open HealthPro Widgets and confirm that the health data you want is enabled.
  • Make sure HealthPro Widgets has permission to read the related data in Apple Health.
  • Open the app after changing permissions or widget settings so the widget can refresh.
  • If necessary, remove the widget and add it again.

Health Data Permissions

HealthPro Widgets only displays Apple Health data that you choose to authorize. You can review or change Health access using the Health app or your device's Privacy & Security settings.

Purchases and Pro Access

If you previously purchased HealthPro Widgets Pro and it is not recognized, open HealthPro Widgets and use the Restore Purchase option. App Store purchases and subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID.
