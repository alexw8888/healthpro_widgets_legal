Private by design

Your health data belongs to you.

HealthPro Widgets is designed to read and display the Apple Health data you choose to authorize. Your health measurements are processed on your device and are not uploaded to a HealthPro Widgets server.

✓No HealthPro account required. Start using the app without creating another health profile online.
✓No advertising use of HealthKit data. Your HealthKit data is not used for advertising, marketing, profiling, or cross-app tracking.
✓You control permissions. Apple shows the health data types requested and lets you grant or revoke access.
